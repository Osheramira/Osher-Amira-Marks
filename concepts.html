Why the Innocence Project is Essential to Modern Justice

The aim of this paper is to examine the role played by the Innocence Project in releasing wrongfully imprisoned people from jail through post-conviction DNA testing and legislation. It will be done by looking at how the Innocence Project exposes judicial failures within the criminal justice system like flawed eyewitness accounts, racism, and debunked forensics.
